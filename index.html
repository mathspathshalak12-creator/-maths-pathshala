    Class-wise details

    What we cover, class by class.

    Open a class to see the subjects, focus areas and teaching approach in detail.

    Class 9 Foundation — PCM Mathematics · Physics · Chemistry
    ▾

    Class 9 is where the board-exam foundation actually gets built — algebra, geometry and number systems in Maths, and the first real introduction to numericals in Physics and Chemistry. We keep the pace steady so concepts are understood, not memorised.

    • Mathematics: number systems, polynomials, coordinate geometry, linear equations, triangles & geometry proofs, circles, mensuration, statistics
    • Physics: motion, force & laws of motion, gravitation, work & energy, sound
    • Chemistry: matter, atoms & molecules, structure of the atom, basic chemical reactions
    • Weekly class tests plus chapter-end tests to catch gaps early, before Class 10 pressure builds up
    • CBSE and ICSE syllabus covered
    Class 10 Board year — PCM Mathematics · Physics · Chemistry
    ▾

    Class 10 is board-exam focused from day one. Every topic is taught with the exam pattern in mind — how it's typically asked, common mistakes, and how to structure a full-marks answer, not just the right answer.

    • Mathematics: real numbers, polynomials, pair of linear equations, quadratic equations, arithmetic progressions, triangles, coordinate geometry, trigonometry & its applications, circles, areas & volumes, statistics & probability
    • Physics: light — reflection & refraction, the human eye, electricity, magnetic effects of current
    • Chemistry: chemical reactions & equations, acids/bases/salts, metals & non-metals, carbon compounds
    • Regular full-length mock tests in the board exam format, with detailed answer-sheet style checking
    • Previous-year question practice and last-minute revision sessions closer to boards
    Class 11 Advanced foundation — Mathematics Mathematics
    ▾

    Class 11 Maths jumps in difficulty compared to Class 10, and it's the base every Class 12 chapter builds on. The focus here is on getting genuinely comfortable with each concept, since rushing this year usually shows up as struggle in Class 12.

    • Sets, relations & functions, trigonometric functions, complex numbers, linear inequalities
    • Permutations & combinations, binomial theorem, sequences & series
    • Straight lines, conic sections, introduction to 3D geometry
    • Limits & derivatives (introduction to calculus), statistics & probability
    • Doubt-clearing built into every session — no concept is left half-understood before moving ahead
    Class 12 Board year — Mathematics Mathematics
    ▾

    Class 12 Maths is taught as direct board-exam preparation, with heavy emphasis on the chapters that carry the most weight every year, plus timed practice so students are exam-ready, not just concept-ready.

    • Relations & functions, inverse trigonometric functions, matrices & determinants
    • Continuity & differentiability, applications of derivatives, integrals & their applications
    • Differential equations, vectors & 3D geometry, linear programming, probability
    • Handwritten, exam-oriented notes for quick revision closer to the exam
    • Full board-pattern mock tests with strict time limits and detailed feedback

    Common questions

    Frequently asked questions.

    Is there a trial class before I enroll? +
    Yes — message us on WhatsApp and we'll set up a free trial class so you can experience the teaching style before committing.
    How and when do I pay the fee? +
    Fees are collected monthly. You can pay via UPI, cash, or bank transfer — details are shared once you register. Your fee status is always visible on your student dashboard.
    What if my child misses a class? +
    Let us know in advance where possible. Missed topics are covered through notes and doubt-clearing in the next session — attendance is tracked on the dashboard so nothing falls through the cracks.
    Do I need to buy separate books or study material? +
    No separate material is required. Handwritten notes are provided for every chapter, and free chapter-wise notes are also available to download from this website.
    Which boards, classes and subjects do you cover? +
    CBSE and ICSE. Our primary programmes are offline and online classes for Class 9–10 PCM and Class 11–12 Mathematics. Home tuition is available separately on request.
